About Yvonne Egbuna

Yvonne Egbuna is a leadership speaker and executive coach who supports corporate leaders and senior women to reclaim their voice, confidence, and authority.

With over twenty years' experience leading leadership development and organisational change across public and private sector organisations, she brings a rare blend of lived experience, emotional intelligence, and senior leadership credibility. Her work is grounded, reflective, and practical, supporting leaders to show up visibly, speak with clarity, and lead with humanity in complex environments.

Yvonne is known for creating spaces where leaders feel safe enough to think differently — and brave enough to lead authentically. Her speaking and coaching combine lived experience with deep organisational insight, helping senior leaders and women in leadership reconnect with their voice, presence, and authority.

Her work is not about performance or polish.
It is about presence, integrity, and leadership that is both effective and deeply human.

Services

How I Work With Organisations

My services are designed to support leaders and organisations navigating change, complexity, and visibility — with a strong emphasis on inclusive leadership and psychological safety.

Keynotes & Executive Talks

I deliver keynote talks and executive briefings for senior leadership audiences, with a focus on inclusive leadership, psychological safety, and advocacy in leadership.

These sessions are reflective, grounded, and practical — designed to provoke thought, surface insight, and support meaningful conversation at leadership level.

They are particularly relevant for organisations seeking to:

  • Strengthen inclusive leadership capability
  • Support women leaders at senior levels
  • Build cultures where people can speak, contribute, and lead openly

Leadership & Organisational Development Programmes

I design and support leadership and organisational development programmes that embed inclusive leadership as a lived practice — not a policy statement.

This work focuses on building high-performing, psychologically safe cultures where leaders are equipped to navigate complexity, difference, and uncertainty with confidence and care.

Programmes are tailored to organisational context and may support:

  • Leadership capability development
  • Culture change initiatives
  • Long-term inclusion and psychological safety goals

Executive Coaching & Leadership Advisory

I offer executive coaching and advisory support for senior leaders and women leaders navigating complexity, transitions, and increased visibility.

This work provides a confidential, reflective space for leaders to:

  • Reconnect with their leadership voice and authority
  • Navigate organisational dynamics and power structures
  • Lead with clarity, confidence, and presence

Coaching is grounded, thoughtful, and responsive to the realities of senior leadership.

Our Approach

Reflective, grounded, and practical

Leadership Identity & Visibility

We explore how you see yourself as a leader and how others experience you. This includes communication presence, executive visibility, and the stories you tell yourself about your leadership.

Psychological Safety

We examine how you create environments where people feel safe to speak up, take risks, and bring their whole selves to work — and how you cultivate this for yourself as well.

Inclusive Leadership

We focus on human-centred leadership practices that honour difference, distribute power thoughtfully, and create conditions where diverse perspectives can thrive.

Organisational Complexity

We navigate the systems, politics, and dynamics that shape leadership effectiveness — helping you work with complexity rather than being overwhelmed by it.
